Reference data on Intermolecular Interactions

In recent years a large amount of very valuable reference data on intermolecular interactions has become available.  Data of this type represents the energy of interaction of two species, A and B, calculated in vacuo.

The procedure for calculating the intermolecular interaction energy using semiempirical methods is rigidly defined.  There are several different ways to do this calculation, but after examining this issue, a method was chosen that is fast and unambiguous and simple.

Method of calculating intermolecular interaction energies

The starting point for each benchmark system is the published geometry of the complex A non-covalently bonded to B.  All benchmark geometries are in Cartesian coordinates, and these coordinates are used without modification in the semiempirical calculations.  A single-point calculation is then done; this gives the heat of formation of the complex.  One of the systems is then moved 50 Angstroms away from its original position.  This effectively deletes any intermolecular interactions.  The modified system is then given the name name as that of the complex, but with the word "separated" or something similar added.  So for water dimer in the S66 set, the complex is given the name "01 Water - Water.mop" and the separated system is given the name "01 Water - Water (Separated).mop" A single-point calculation is then done on the separated system; this gives the heat of formation of the complex. 

Using these two heats of formation, the intermolecular interaction energy is then defined as (heat of formation of the complex) minus (heat of formation of the separated system).

Complications

Some systems are charged, in which case the MOPAC keyword CHARGE has to be used.  Some systems consist of more than two species, in cases like that a visual inspection of the complex provides information on which intermolecular interaction is being referred to.

Caveats

The systems are not allowed to relax, so the results obtained are specific to the geometries provided by the benchmark publication.  The calculated intermolecular interaction energies could not be calculated unless the benchmark geometries were used.
