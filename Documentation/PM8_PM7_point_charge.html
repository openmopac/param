PM8 should have PM7's point charge approximation

 

PM7 is able to model solids with much higher accuracy than PM6 because in PM7 the two-electron two center integrals converge on the exact integral at about 6.5 Ångstroms while the equivalent PM6 integrals are consistently smaller than the exact values. Although the effect of individual perturbations of this type was small, the effect on solids would be infinite, see Fig. 1 in the original PM7 publication.  The change in definition of these integrals thus represents a definite improvement in PM7 over PM6.

In the design of PM8, the starting point was PM6-D3H4.  The D3H4 approximation is significantly better than that in PM7.  However, the small change in the two-electron two center integrals in PM7 should be made to PM8.

This has not been done. As soon as it is done, this web-page will be updated.
