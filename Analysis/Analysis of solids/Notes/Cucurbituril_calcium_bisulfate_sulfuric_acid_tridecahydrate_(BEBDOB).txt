The formula given in the CSD is (C<sub>36</sub>H<sub>36</sub>N<sub>24</sub>O<sub>12</sub>), 2[Ca]<sup>2+</sup>, 4[HO<sub>4</sub>S]<sup>-</sup>, 13(H<sub>2</sub>O), but the positions of the hydrogen atoms
on the water and sulfate moieties were not defined.  The species used at the start of the geometry optimization were
the cucurbituril, four sulfate dianions, four hydroxonium cations, and nine water molecules, in addition to the two 
calcium ions.  When the optimization finished, three protons had migrated from the hydroxonium ions to the sulfate ions to give
three hydrogen sulfate anions, one sulfate dianion, one hydroxonium, and 12 water molecules.  The single hydroxonium 
ion was located inside the cucurbituril ring.  No attempt has been made to determine if this is the lowest energy 
system, or whether four hydrogen sulfate anions and 13 water molecules would be more stable.
