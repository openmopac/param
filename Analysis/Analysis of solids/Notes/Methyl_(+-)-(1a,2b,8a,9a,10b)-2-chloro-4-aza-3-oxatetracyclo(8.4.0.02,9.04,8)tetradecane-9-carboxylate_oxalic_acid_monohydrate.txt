Notes:  Methyl (+-)-(1a,2b,8a,9a,10b)-2-chloro-4-aza-3-oxatetracyclo(8.4.0.02,9.04,8)
tetradecane-9-carboxylate oxalic acid monohydrate is an example of where PM6 incorrectly
predicts the hydrogen bonding.

Look carefully at the oxalic acid moieties. In the X-Ray structure, one end of
each acid is neutral, and the other is hydrogen bonded to a nitrogen.  PM6 predicts
that the hydrogen at the neutral end forms a bridging bond with the nearby water.
