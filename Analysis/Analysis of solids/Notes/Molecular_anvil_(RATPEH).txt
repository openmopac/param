In this systen, 9,10-bis(3,5-Dihydroxyphenyl)anthracene bis(ethyl acrylate) 
cyclohexa-1,5-diene clathrate, the ethyl acrylate is firmly held in place by
the "anvil," an anthracenebisresorcinol derivative. 

Although the chemical name includes cyclohexa-1,5-diene, the X-ray structure
only has benzene, albeit somewhat distorted.  It's not apparent from the
data where the two extra hydrogen atoms go.  Or for that matter, why the
ring is not cyclohexa-1,3-diene.
