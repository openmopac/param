This is one of the solids used in the blind tests of crystal structure prediction. 
See: "Significant progress in predicting the crystal structures of small organic molecules – a report on the fourth blind test", Acta Crys. B 865, 107-125 (2009), and 
"Towards crystal structure prediction of pharmaceutically relevant organic molecules – a report on the fifth blind test", Bardwell, et. al (submitted)<BR>
The complete set consists of <a href="../CSP_Blind_Test.html">22 solids</a>.
