Notes: In this crystal there are three oxalic acid molecules for every two pyridine molecules.
Both pyridines are ionized, so that leaves four protons to be shared by three oxalates.  This
results in a chain of oxalate - (C2O4)-H-(C2O4)- plus one branch occurs every three oxalates.

The precursers to this co-crystal are <a href="Pyridine__PYRDNA03__jmol.html">Pyridine</a> and <a href="Oxalic_acid__OXALAC06__jmol.html">Oxalic acid</a>
