When the geometry in the ICSD for lead oxide hydrate, entry 20701, was used to start the PM7 optimization,
the resulting structure had a higher heat of formation than that expected.
This was traced back to possibly incorrect locations of the hydrogen atoms in the X-ray structure.  
The hydrogen atoms were moved to what appeared to be more chemically sensible positions, and their positions optimized.  
The resulting structure was then used as the starting point for the PM7 calculation.
