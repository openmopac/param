Sodium iodide crystallizes from acetone as the tri-acetone sodium iodide.  
There is room in the lattice for extra iodine atoms.  If these are added,
the formula changes from [Na(C3H6O)3I] to [Na(C3H6O)3I2].  Does the extra
iodine atom exist as the radical or in some other form?
