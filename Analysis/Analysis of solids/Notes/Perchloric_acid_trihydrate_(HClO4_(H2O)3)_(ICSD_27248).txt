This system could better be described as [H7O3][ClO4].
The ion H7O3 can be identified by first locating the hydronium or oxonium ion, H3O(+),
then finding the two water molecules near to it that form short hydrogen bonds.
Some ions, e.g., the oxonium containing O56, are not complete, because a water molecule 
is in the next cluster.
