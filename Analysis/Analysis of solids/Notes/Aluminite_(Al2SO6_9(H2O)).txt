Aluminite can be regarded as a chain of AlO6 octahedra sharing either a face or edge. 
This system illustrates aluminum bonded to both water and hydroxyl.  For aluminum
bonded to hydroxyl only, see <a href="Bayerite__Al_OH_3__jmol.html">bayerite</a> and <a href="Gibbsite__Al_OH_3__jmol.html">gibbsite</a>, bonded to water only, see the <a href="Ammonium_aluminum_sulfate___NH4_Al_SO4_2__12_H2O___jmol.html">aluminum alums</a>,
and for Al bonded to oxygen, see <a href="Diaspore__AlO_OH___jmol.html">diaspore</a>.
