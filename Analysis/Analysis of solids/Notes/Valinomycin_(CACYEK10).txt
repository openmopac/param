There is a fault in the implementation of translational vectors in very large unit cells such as valinomycin.
This is not an error in theory, or a software error, instead it is an implementation error.

Possible ways to correct this error are:
(a) Increase the force constant for the translation vectors. This would reduce the step-size.
(b) Do not allow the heat of formation to rise (in the L-BFGS method, the energy can rise)
