In this complex, the dithiolene moiety is a "non-innocent ligand." A simple way to
see the complexity of this ligand is to work out the formal oxidation state of the
central vanadium atom.  Depending on how the Lewis structure is drawn, the vanadium
atom has an oxidation state of zero (with S=CH-HC=S), or six (with -S-CH=HC-S-), but
no simple structure can give a reasonable value of V(v).
