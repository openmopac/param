Notes:  This heteropolyoxometallate system has the largest unit cell that has been completely 
optimized using PM6. Its formula is C272 H352 N28 O80 Si2 Mo24 Br4 Zn4, and consists of two keggins,
each composed of 12 molybdenum trioxide fragments forming a cage, inside which is a SiO4.

There are four porphorin dianion rings each with a zinc atom at the center and a bromide ion sitting
above the ring.  To balance the charges, there are 12 tetraethylammonium cations.  

The formal charges for one mer (one keggin, two porphorins, and six tetraethylammonium ions)
can be expressed as:

 +0:   Mo12O36 cage.
 -4:   SiO4(4-)
 -2:   two porphorins
 +6:   six tetraethylammonium ions

In the cluster used, one silicate tetranion is positioned inside a cage, the other is in free space.
This is a consequence of the cell generator - the periodic boundary conditions make the two silicates
equivalent
