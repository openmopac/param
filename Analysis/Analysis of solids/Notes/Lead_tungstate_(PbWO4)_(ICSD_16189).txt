This structure, published in 1970, contains planar PbO4 and planar WO4 units.
The reference geometry has a very high heat of formation compared to the optimized structure.

A related system is <a href="Lead_tungstate__PbWO4___ICSD_81550__jmol.html">Lead tungstate (PbWO4) (ICSD 81550)</a>, in which the more common tetrahedral WO4 group is present. 
For this system, the heat of formation of the optimized geometry is much nearer to that of the reference geometry, suggesting that the X-ray structure is more reasonable.
