Enstatite and perovskite are polymorphs of MgSiO3. At low pressures enstatite is the more stable structure,
with silicon tetrahedrally coordinated by four oxygen atoms, and magnesium octahedrally coordinated by six
atoms. This means that each oxygen is tetrahedrally coordinated to one silicon and three magnesium atoms.

At high pressure, perovskite is the more stable polymorph, with each silicon atom octahedrally coordinated,
and each magnesium trigonal pyramidally coordinated to three oxygen atoms. Each oxygen is trigonally coordinated
by one magnesium and two silicon atoms. The formal bond-order from oxygen to each ligand is 2/3.
