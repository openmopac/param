In this system, there is a proton between a nitrogen of the imidazole and an oxygen of the
carboxylate. The N - O distance is unusually short: X-Ray gives 2.465 Angstroms.

This system is also interesting in that the close N - O distance cannot be reproduced when
a molecule is calculated in isolation - the imidazole and pyridine rings twist out of plane.
