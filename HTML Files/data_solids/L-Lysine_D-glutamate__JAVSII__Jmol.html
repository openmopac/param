In the solid state, amino-acids exist as Zwitterions. The Zwitterionic form is stabilized by the close
proximity of the -COO(-) and -NH3(+) ions in each moiety - they are always only separated by one -CHR- group.
So would isolated amine and carboxylic acid groups also be stabilized? In the co-crystal lysine - glutamate
each terminal ionizable group can only be stabilized by the counterion group on another molecule.

In this system, the anion - cation - Zwitterion is the stable form.
