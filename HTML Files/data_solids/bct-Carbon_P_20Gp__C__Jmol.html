Graphite can be converted into diamond by compressing it. Intermediate between graphite and diamond is a
transparent allotrope with a body-centered tetragonal lattice: bct-Carbon. This system has been modeled,
"Ab initio study of the formation of transparent carbon under pressure", X.-F. Zhou, X. Dong, G.-R. Qian,
L. Zhang, Y. Tian, H.-T. Wang, Phys. Rev. B 82, 134126 (2010). The lattice parameters at 20GPa are:
a = b = 4.322 Å and c = 2.478 Å.
